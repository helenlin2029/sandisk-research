**ITEM 1: BUSINESS**

**Independence:** <br>
Granted Feb. 2025 following separation from Western Digital Corporation <br>
Publicly traded under ticker SNDK <br> 
Certain products will also be sold for a limited transitional period under the Western Digital®, WD® and other brands under license from WDC <br>


**Market:** <br>
Hardware & Tech (specifically data storage): data storage devices and solution based on NAND technology <br>
**Products:** <br>
Solid state drives, embedded products, removable cards, universal serial bus drives and wafers and components <br>
**Consumers:** <br>
Data centers, edge device manufacturers (OEMs), general consumers (cloud --> client --> consumer) <br>
    - Cloud: "large and growing end market comprised primarily of products for public or private cloud environments and enterprise customers" (AI-related workloads, data analysis and other enterprise applications) <br>
    - Client: "provide numerous data solutions that we incorporate into our client’s devices, which consist of solid state drive desktop and notebook PCs, gaming consoles and set top boxes, as well as flash-based embedded storage products for mobile phones, tablets, notebook PCs and other portable and wearable devices, automotive applications, Internet of Things, and industrial and connected home applications" <br>
    - Consumer: "provide portfolio of solid state drives and removable flash, including cards and universal serial bus flash drives" <br>


**Driving Market Demands**:** <br>
Development in AI and cloud infrastructure/computing <br>
**What they do:** <br>
Develop and manufacture (low cost, high performance) solid state storage products (including research) <br>
**Competitors:** <br>
Vertically integrated suppliers such as Kioxia, Micron Technology, Inc., Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd., SK Hynix, Inc., Yangtze Memory Technologies Co., Ltd. and numerous smaller companies that assemble flash into products <br>
**Business Strategy:** <br>
Innovation and Cost Leadership (meeting market demands in terms of efficiency, scaling, performance) & Broad Product Portfolio <br>


**IP:** <br>
NDAs, ~7,900 granted patents and ~3,200 pending patents, cross licenses and agreements for external companies, fear of less robust confidential information laws in foreign jurisdictions <br>


**Operations:** <br>
    - All flash-based memory is obtained from Sandisk's joint ventures with Kioxia (wafers) & less often, from other manufacturers <br>
    - Assembly and test operations comprise in-house assembly and test facilities located in Penang, Malaysia and other contract manufacturers, and the assembly and test facility owned and operated by SDSS Venture (SDSS is 20% owned by Sandisk and 80% owned by JCET Management Co., Ltd.) <br>
    - Sandisk and Kioxia currently operate three business ventures, Flash Partners Ltd., Flash Alliance Ltd. and Flash Forward Ltd., (collectively, “Flash Ventures”) across seven flash-based manufacturing facilities in Japan (Flash Ventures will begin flash-based manufacturing operations at an eighth facility in Japan in calendar year 2025). <br>
    - Sandisk and Kioxia are entitled to purchase a share of Flash Ventures’ output, which generally equals 50% each. The price for which they pay Flash Ventures for flash memory wafers is cost plus a small markup. Sandisk is obligated to pay for variable costs incurred in producing our share of Flash Ventures’ flash-based memory wafer supply based on a rolling forecast. Sandisk is obligated to pay for half of Flash Ventures’ fixed costs regardless of the output Sandisk chooses to purchase. <br>
    - While Flash Ventures is operating, Sandisk and Kioxia are restricted from working with third parties to manufacture flash-based memory or from fabricating flash-based memory beyond the capacity specified in agreements with Kioxia. Sandisk may not manufacture flash-based memory except to the extent that Sandisk acquires the manufacturing capacity of a Flash Ventures entity. <br>
    - Sandisk jointly invests with Kioxia in the manufacturing equipment needed to support Flash Ventures’ flash manufacturing operations and are obligated to fund 49.9% to 50.0% of capital investments that a Flash Ventures entity decides to make to the extent that the Flash Ventures entity’s operating cash flow is insufficient to fund these investments. <br>
    - Agreements: Flash Partners Ltd. and Flash Alliance Ltd. (Dec 31, 2029) and Flash Forward Ltd. (Dec 31 2034) <br>
    - On January 24, 2025, the Company and WDC entered into an equity transfer agreement (the “Equity Transfer Agreement”) to transfer WDC’s entire equity interest in Unisplendour Corporation Limited and Unissoft (Wuxi) Group Co. Ltd. (“Unis”), referred to as the “Unis Venture,” to the Company. The Unis Venture is 48% owned by the Company and 52% owned by Unis. The Unis Venture markets and sells the Company’s products in China. <br>


**Sales and Distribution:** <br>
    - International sales, which include sales to foreign subsidiaries of U.S. companies but do not include sales to U.S. subsidiaries of foreign companies, represented 80%, 86% and 81% of net revenue for 2025, 2024 and 2023, respectively. <br>
    - For 2025 and 2024, no customer accounted for more than 10% of net revenue. For 2023, one customer accounted for 15% of net revenue. <br>
    - Higher levels of demand in the first and second fiscal quarters as a result of increased customer spending. <br>
